# Problem Statement and Scope

## Problem statement

Malaysian students and early-career job seekers who know their target career may still not know how to close their skill gaps. MYFutureJobs can show the skills required for a chosen occupation, but its career-path view does not, in the available jobseeker manual, give a detailed learning path for each missing skill (MYFutureJobs, 2023). This matters because users need to decide what knowledge, tools and prerequisites to learn first, not only see a list of skills. DOSM reported that 35.2% of employed persons with tertiary education were in skill-related underemployment in Q2 2026 (DOSM, 2026).

## Users and stakeholders

- Students and fresh graduates: understand gaps for a target entry-level career.
- Early-career job seekers: update skills and receive a revised learning order.
- Career advisers: use explanations as a starting point for guidance.
- MYFutureJobs/PERKESO: improve career-support information.
- Universities, training providers and occupation experts: supply reviewed skill information.

## In scope

- Choose a supported entry-level career profile.
- Enter self-ratings for required skills.
- Show gaps, priority, knowledge topics, tools, prerequisites and learning order.
- Update the plan after a skill rating changes.
- Use a small reviewed knowledge base for a prototype.

## Out of scope

- Live vacancy matching, applications and hiring decisions.
- CV scraping or automatic skill verification.
- Course payment, enrolment or certificates.
- A job guarantee or replacement for human career counselling.
